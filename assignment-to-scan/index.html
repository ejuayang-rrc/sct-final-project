Our Founder

Richie William Richardson III

Since the year 1877, the Richardson family has been one who always stood for the people of the communities they surround themselves with.
Our founder, Sir-Richie William Richardson III, has gone beyond his forefathers to support communities across the world! Not only supporting his local community, but those he may yet know.

A quote from our founding father, Sir-Richie William Richardson III himself:

"I, like my father before me, have always stood for my community. We find ourselves fighting every day to stand for ourselves, believing that we can take on the world with our own set of strong kickers, when what we really need is someone else to help bring us up with them, benefitting everyone. My goal has ever been and always will be, to support those in need."

Elijah Juayang • Steffen Walker • Braden Warren
